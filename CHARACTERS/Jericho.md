Attended the Magic School of Neverwinter, in his various classes he briefly met an Arakocra named Leerreza. They had Spellcasting together and were grouped up as a duo together in group projects, but neither of them were successful when it came to casting spells. One day Leerreza dropped out of the school to pursue archery. Later that night Jericho went on one of his usual midnight strolls in the forest by the campus. As he was admiring the full moon he was struck upon lightning, he fell down and was briefly paralyzed. Once he regained consciousness he stood up and raised his forearm to his snout to cover a sneeze. As he sneezed, sparks flung out of his finger tips and his eyes widened. Jericho then gets a thought in his head, he takes one step back and aims at a nearby tree and attempts to cast eldritch blast on it. To his surprise, he actually manages to strike it with magic. As he returns to his dorm as he's changing into his sleepwear, he notices through the floor length mirror that he now has a big scar in the shape of a crescent moon on his back. He's fully convinced that it wasn't lightning that game him the mark, but instead that it's a blessing from the moon that is allowing him to now cast magic. He remembers when he was a youngling that his mother picked the name Jericho because it was the Ibixian name for the city of the moon. He's a suspicious creature and is always snooping around areas he isn't supposed to be in at the campus. On his way to sneak into the admin's office, he sees a giant goliath talking to the admin. The goliath named Sly is inquiring about the constellations under their eye and their meaning. The admin reveals that their constellations professor left a few days ago to Phandalin. The admin reveals that someone named Gundren Rockseeker may know about their whereabouts as they have a delivery for them in the city. After the admin leaves, Jericho introduces himself to Sly and asks if he can tag along to seek Gundren. Jericho feels it's no coincidence that the moon and now constellations are guiding him to a new journey. Jericho and Sly stumble across Gundren. He then tasks Jericho and Sly to bring a wagonload of supplies to the town of Phandalin from Neverwinter. As he sets foot onto the wagon he seems some familiar feathers, a tiny tabaxi, a tall loxodon, and to his surprise the popular bard of Neverwinter: Gnomebrina.
