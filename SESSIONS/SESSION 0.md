**Roleplaying is FUN! at least TRY it!**
We're also able to *skip* cutscenes if you don't feel down to roleplay a scene out if you'd like

**We will have a running initiative list, and Jess will let you know when your turn is coming up next!** Announce your intent before you do it to keep combat running smooth! Also, announce and DC's that are required for saving throws

**Roll damage when you roll your attack!** If it hits, we ignore it but it saves quite a bit of time if it's already known.


**Speaking During Combat:** Each combat turn lasts about 6 seconds. On their turn (with some leniency), your character may say or shout a brief sentence in that time. Try to be efficient, and not have a full blown conversation in the midst of combat.


**Dice Rolls Against Other Players:** Unless the DM calls for it (or if both players request it), please do not make dice rolls against your fellow players, i.e. for persuasion, etc. 

**PVP/Player-Character Conflict:** Conflict between characters sometimes happens, but I personally as a DM would prefer if we tried to keep things civil.

**Share the Spotlight**

**Player-Character Secrets:** Some characters may have secrets that they keep from others. If you do have a secret, please let me know what that secret is.

**Lines & Veils:** We’ll talk more about this in a second, but player/DM comfort and safety come before everything. Lines and Veils should be respected, always, and if the DM or another player requests a break, or a shift in topic, we will respect that request.

**Murder Hoboing Discouraged:** Since we’re trying to experience a true taste of a fantasy campaign, murder hoboing is discouraged. This is when players try to meet every interaction with combat, doing things like murdering the innkeeper because he overcharged you for a room, etc. We’re operating under the assumption that casual murder is not commonplace. 


**Meta-Gaming**: Don't use knowledge that your character wouldn't have. Wizards wouldn't search a chest after a rogue did, even if the player knows they rogue missed a trap.

**Encumbrance:** Not going to measure EVERYTHING, just don't abuse it and make it realistic.

**Material Components:** - generally don't matter unless they're expensive

**Each Long Rest will start with a Short Rest** 
(also, don't LONG REST until DM let's us know we can)

**Attunement:** Attuning to an item will count as if you’ve also cast the Identify spell. When you attune to a magical item, you learn all its properties.  

**Repeated Rolls** Failing a roll opens up a party member to help *on occasion*. They'll need to describe how they are going to help. Up to DM descretion.

**DEATH SAVES** - privately rolled, 1 per turn and sent to DM privately.
1-9 FAIL
10-20 PASS
Nat 1 = 2 fails
Nat 20 = character returns to life with 1hp
**3 Failures: You’re dead  
3 Successes: You stabilize, but are unconscious**
